# 5.2 — Reports: ORD-Specific

## Report: Application by Risk Level
- Source: REMIS `Assigned Risk Level` field (`3.2`)
- Grouping: Minimal / Moderate / High
- Filters: date range, department, study type
- Output: table + pie chart

## Report: Reviewer Workload
- Source: REMIS reviewer assignments (`3.3`)
- Metrics: # active assigned applications per reviewer, # completed reviews per period,
  average review turnaround time
- Filters: date range, reviewer, risk track
- Output: table sortable by workload

## Report: Annual Ethics Report
- Source: All REMIS records for the selected year
- Metrics: total applications submitted/approved/rejected, breakdown by risk level, by
  department, by study type; monitoring compliance summary; archived studies count
- Filters: year
- Output: formatted summary document (PDF), suitable for institutional annual reporting

## Report: Archive Studies Report
- Source: REMIS records with status = Completed/Archived (`3.4`)
- Metrics: list of archived studies with final outcome, archive date, PI, department
- Filters: date range, department, final outcome
- Output: table; export PDF/Excel
