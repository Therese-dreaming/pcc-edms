## Administrative Screening (FRS §VI — performed by Ethics Secretariat)
Performed after academic endorsement (`3.3`) and before risk classification, to confirm
application completeness/eligibility.

### Checklist (source: FRS §VI, confirmed)
- [ ] Proposal Attached
- [ ] Consent Form Attached
- [ ] Instrument Attached
- [ ] Signatures Complete
- [ ] Required Templates Used

### Screening Decision (source: FRS §VI, confirmed)
- [ ] Complete
- [ ] Incomplete
- [ ] Returned for Compliance

> The system must **automatically generate a deficiency notice** when an application is marked
> Incomplete or Returned for Compliance (FRS §VI, confirmed — not optional).

## Risk Classification (FRS §VII — performed by Ethics Reviewer, confirmed by Ethics Committee Chair)

> **2026-09-05 (decision — no longer open):** exact threshold criteria between Minimal / Moderate /
> High **do not matter and will not be codified**. Both screening and risk classification are
> **decision buttons** — the reviewer/secretariat exercises judgement and records the outcome; the
> system does not auto-classify or enforce thresholds. The criteria column below is illustrative
> guidance only, not a rule the software applies.

| Level | Review Type Assigned | Criteria (ASSUMPTION — FRS names the three levels and their review tracks but not the exact criteria thresholds; confirm with REC) |
|---|---|---|
| Minimal Risk | Expedited Review | No sensitive data, no vulnerable population, standard anonymized survey/interview |
| Moderate Risk | Committee Review | Sensitive personal data, or vulnerable population, but standard mitigations apply |
| High Risk | Full Board Review | Vulnerable population + sensitive data, invasive procedures, deception, or significant harm potential |

## Risk Classification Fields on Record (FRS §VII, confirmed: Classifier, Date, Rationale)
| Field | Type |
|---|---|
| Assigned Risk Level | Dropdown (Minimal/Moderate/High) |
| Review Type Assigned | Auto-derived from Risk Level (Expedited/Committee/Full Board) |
| Classified By | User reference (Ethics Reviewer) |
| Classification Date | Date |
| Classification Rationale | Text area |

This field is the primary data source for the **"Application by Risk Level"** report
(`5.2-reports-ord.md`).
