## 1. Understanding Summary

**PCC-EDMS** is a single platform shared by two offices with distinct legal/procedural
obligations that happen to need the same infrastructure:

- **DPO** owns two modules: **DPREQ** (Module 1 — pre-research data privacy clearance) and
  **DPNDA** (Module 2 — e-signed NDAs for OJTs/trainees/community service). Both are
  single-approval-chain workflows: an applicant submits, DPO staff screens and reviews, DPO
  issues a clearance or signs off. DPNDA additionally tracks *placement* data (school,
  department, grade level, dates) that's the backbone of most DPO reports — not just a signed
  PDF.

- **ORD, via REC,** owns **REMIS** (Module 3), which is structurally heavier than DPREQ/DPNDA:
  a researcher's application must clear a **four-step academic endorsement chain** (Adviser →
  Program Head → Dean) *before* it ever reaches the Ethics Secretariat for administrative
  screening, then goes through risk-tiered review (Expedited/Committee/Full Board, per
  classification), a four-way decision (Approved / Approved with Conditions / Deferred /
  Disapproved), ongoing **monitoring** once approved, an explicit **Completion Module** to
  close it out, and a standalone **Incident Reporting** pathway (`3.5`, newly added — see below)
  that can fire at any point during monitoring, independent of the main status lifecycle.

- **4.x (shared infrastructure)** is what makes "single platform" true rather than aspirational:
  one user/role model, one file-naming and repository convention, one e-signature and
  notification mechanism, one audit trail. Every module writes into these rather than
  maintaining its own.

- **5.x (reporting)** is downstream of everything above — every report is defined as "read this
  field from that module's records." The reports are only as reliable as the underlying data
  model, which is why `5.3`'s note about needing a first-class `Placement` entity (not just a
  signed NDA file) mattered enough to carry into `system-design.md`.

**The DPO ↔ REMIS integration point** (this is the piece the original request specifically
asked about): the two offices are otherwise independent, but a REMIS incident of type **Data
Breach** or **Confidentiality Breach** (`3.5`) now explicitly notifies **DPO Staff**, because a
privacy breach inside a REC-cleared study is a DPO matter under the Data Privacy Act regardless
of which office's system it surfaced in. This is modeled as a single cross-module edge in
`knowledge-graph.json` (`entity.incident --NOTIFIES--> role.dpo_staff`) rather than a merge of
the two modules — DPREQ/DPNDA and REMIS keep separate ownership, statuses, and approval chains;
they share infrastructure and this one notification path, not their workflows.

**What changed in this pass, and why it mattered:** the original `3.1`–`3.4` were a reasonable
draft written *before* `REMIS_Functional_Requirements_Specification.md` was available, and
diverged from it in ways that weren't cosmetic — the endorsement chain, the Ethics
Secretariat/Reviewer/Chair role split, and the entire Incident Reporting module were absent.
Building the architecture on the old draft would have produced a system that couldn't represent
how REMIS actually needs to work. Full diff in `CHANGELOG.md`.

## 2a. DPO ↔ Ethics Integration (added 2026-07-02, second pass)

`reqs/` (4 EVP-approved DPO EForm PDFs, previously not incorporated into this doc set) revealed
that DPREQ and REMIS are not independent applications each producing their own clearance — they
are two internal review tracks acting on one shared application (Form 1), converging on one
dual-signed clearance certificate (Form 3). Full model: `0.4-dpo-ethics-integration.md`.
Confirmed directly by the requester, not an assumption. This changed `1.1`–`1.3`, `2.1`–`2.2`,
`3.1`, `3.3`, and `system-design.md` §3/§4 — see `CHANGELOG.md` for the itemized diff.

New open questions this raised:
- 🔴 Does every Form 1 submission require the *full* REMIS track (endorsement chain → Ethics
  Secretariat screening → risk classification → committee review), or only some studies? Assumed
  "every submission, both tracks" until ORD/Ethics Secretariat confirms otherwise.
- 🔴 Is Form 3's "Research Ethics Head" the same role as the FRS's "Ethics Committee Chair," or a
  distinct, more senior signatory? Assumed same role, two labels, until ORD confirms.
- 🔴 Does Form 2 (Research Team NDA) apply to every research application with human respondents,
  including solo researchers, or only team submissions? Assumed required for all, since Form 1
  supports a 1-person "group."
