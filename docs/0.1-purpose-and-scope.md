# 0.1 — Purpose and Scope

## System Name
PCC Electronic Document Management System (PCC-EDMS)

## Owning Offices
| Office | Role |
|---|---|
| Data Privacy Office (DPO) | Owns Module 1 (DPREQ) and Module 2 (DPNDA) |
| Office of Research and Development (ORD), via Research Ethics Committee (REC) | Owns Module 3 (REMIS) |

## Scope Statement
PCC-EDMS is a single digital platform, shared by DPO and ORD/REC, that provides:

| # | Capability | Primarily serves |
|---|---|---|
| a | Online application for DPO and REC clearances | Both |
| b | Online submission of requirements | Both |
| c | Online signing and submission of NDAs for researchers and OJTs | DPO |
| d | Digital repository of documents | Both |
| e | Workflow automation | Both |
| f | Report generation | Both |

## Out of Scope (ASSUMPTION — confirm with stakeholders)
The source requirements do not explicitly exclude anything. The following are assumed
out of scope unless stated otherwise:
- Financial/budget management for research grants
- HR/payroll systems for OJTs
- Learning management (LMS) functions for student teachers
- Public-facing research publication/portal features

## Success Criteria (ASSUMPTION — for validation, not in source doc)
- 100% of DPREQ and REMIS applications processed without paper forms
- All OJT/community-service NDAs signed and stored electronically
- All required reports (see `5.x` files) generatable on demand without manual data compilation
