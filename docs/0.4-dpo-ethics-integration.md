# 0.4 — DPO ↔ Ethics (REMIS) Integration Model

> **⚠️ UPDATED 2026-07-25 — Independent certificate issuance (stakeholder decision).** The
> stakeholders reversed the joint-clearance model: DPO and Ethics now each issue their **own,
> independent** clearance certificate. DPO approval issues the Data Privacy (DPREQ) clearance
> immediately; the Ethics decision issues the Research Ethics (REMIS) clearance immediately.
> **Neither waits for the other, and there is no longer a dual-signature release gate.** The
> rationale: an applicant may satisfy one office's requirements but not the other's, and each
> department must be able to clear its own side on its own timeline. Sections below marked
> *(superseded 2026-07-25)* describe the retired joint model, kept for history. See
> `stakeholder-additional-features.md` and `HANDOFF.md` (2026-07-25 entry) for the full change.
>
> **What this means concretely:** one `clearance_certificates` row per `research_application`
> still exists, but it now carries **two independent issuances** — `dpreq_*` (certificate number,
> issued_at, valid_until, qr_token, pdf) set on DPO approval, and `remis_*` set on the Ethics
> decision. Each is released to the applicant the moment its own side signs. The shared
> `research_applications.overall_status` reads `partially_cleared` when exactly one side has
> issued, `clearance_issued` only when both have. Each certificate carries its own auto-generated,
> never-reused **Control Number** (`DPREQ-2026-000145` / `REMIS-2026-000098`), searchable and used
> for public verification (each control number resolves to exactly one track).

> **New file, added 2026-07-02.** Written after reconciling `reqs/DPO EFORM 1/2/3/5 SAMPLE.pdf`
> — four EVP-approved PCC forms not previously incorporated into this doc set — against
> `REMIS_Functional_Requirements_Specification.md`. The two sources initially looked
> contradictory (see `CHANGELOG.md` for the full reconciliation note); this file records the
> resolved model. **Confirmed with the requester** (not an ASSUMPTION): there are two
> departments, DPO and Ethics (REC/ORD). REMIS covers Ethics processes only — DPO is a separate,
> parallel track that this pass adds explicit integration points for.

## The resolved model (current — independent issuance, 2026-07-25)

A research application has **one intake, two internal review tracks, two independent outputs**:

```
                         Form 1 (single application)
                                    │
                    ┌───────────────┴───────────────┐
                    ▼                                 ▼
            DPO track (1.x)                   Ethics track (3.x / REMIS)
     DPO screening → review → approval    Endorsement chain → Ethics Secretariat
     (+ Form 2: Research Team NDA,        screening → risk classification →
       DPO-POL-005, if a team)            Ethics Reviewer review → Committee decision
                    │                                 │
                    ▼                                 ▼
   Data Privacy Clearance              Research Ethics Clearance
   (DPO Officer signs)                 (Research Ethics Head signs)
   Control No. DPREQ-YYYY-NNNNNN       Control No. REMIS-YYYY-NNNNNN
   Issued & downloadable the moment    Issued & downloadable the moment
   the DPO side approves               the Ethics side decides
                    └── independent, no cross-dependency ──┘
```

> *(superseded 2026-07-25)* — the retired model had **one joint output**: a single Form 3
> certificate co-signed by both offices and withheld until both signatures existed. It was
> reversed by stakeholder decision (see the banner at the top of this file).

## What this resolves

- **Why REMIS's elaborate endorsement chain (Adviser → Program Head → Dean), Ethics
  Secretariat/Reviewer/Chair split, and risk tiering don't appear anywhere on Form 1:** they
  aren't researcher-facing. Form 1 is the single front-door intake; the FRS-derived process in
  `3.2`/`3.3` is the Ethics side's **internal back-office handling** of that same submission.
  DPO's own screening/review (`1.2`) is the DPO side's internal back-office handling of the
  identical submission, running in parallel. Neither track is a separate application the
  researcher fills out twice.
- **Why there are two certificates (as of 2026-07-25):** the two tracks are independent approval
  chains, and the stakeholders confirmed each office must clear its own side independently — an
  applicant may satisfy DPO requirements but not Ethics, or vice versa. Each track therefore
  issues and releases its own certificate on its own timeline.

## Issuance rule (current — system-enforced, 2026-07-25)

Each side releases its own certificate the moment its own signature lands:

- **DPO track:** DPO approval sets `dpreq_certificate_number` (a fresh Control Number),
  `dpreq_issued_at`, `dpreq_valid_until`, `dpreq_qr_token`, generates the Data Privacy Clearance
  PDF, and transitions the DPREQ application to `clearance_issued`. Downloadable immediately.
- **Ethics track:** an approving Ethics decision does the same with the `remis_*` columns,
  generates the Research Ethics Clearance PDF, and transitions the REMIS application to
  `clearance_issued` (then auto-starts `monitoring`, per `3.4`). Downloadable immediately.

Enforced in `App\Shared\Clearance\Services\ClearanceService` (each `signDpoTrack()` /
`signEthicsTrack()` is idempotent and issues independently), not in controllers.

> *(superseded 2026-07-25)* — the retired **dual-signature gate** read: "The system must not make
> the clearance certificate PDF downloadable, nor mark it issued, until **both** `dpo_signed_by`
> and `ethics_signed_by` are populated." That gate implemented the requester's earlier
> instruction ("Clearance should not be accessible by the researchers if only one signature is
> signed"), which the 2026-07-25 stakeholder decision explicitly overrides.

## Open questions this raises (add to `9.1`'s consolidated list)

- 🔴 **Does every Form-1 submission require the full REMIS track** (endorsement chain → Ethics
  Secretariat screening → risk classification → committee review), or only studies meeting some
  threshold (e.g., involving human subjects beyond a certain risk level)? Form 1's checklist
  (minors, harm/risk, confidentiality) reads as a screening trigger, not proof every study needs
  the full committee process — needs ORD/Ethics Secretariat confirmation. Until answered, this
  doc set assumes **every application goes through both tracks**.
- 🟢 **Role title reconciliation — confirmed 2026-07-04 by the requester:** "Research Ethics
  Head" (Form 3) and "Ethics Committee Chair" (FRS/`0.2`/`3.x`) are one person under two labels,
  not two distinct signatories. No code change needed — `joint-clearance.blade.php` already
  prints the applicant-facing "Research Ethics Head" label while the system internally tracks the
  signatory as the `ethics_committee_chair` role; this confirms that existing split is correct as-is.
- 🔴 **Form 2 applicability:** Form 1's checklist item 3 ("voluntary participation") and the NDA
  purpose statement suggest Form 2 (Research Team NDA) is required for *all* research
  applications with human respondents, not just some — confirm whether solo-researcher
  applications still require Form 2 (the form supports a 1-person "group").

## What did *not* change

- Form 5 (OJT NDA) and the `Placement` entity (`system-design.md` §3.3) are unaffected by this
  integration — OJT/trainee placements never touch the Ethics track, only DPO.
- Module ownership is unchanged: DPO still owns `1.x`/`2.x` data, Ethics/ORD still owns `3.x`
  data. What's shared is the parent `research_applications` record; the two departments'
  review authority was never merged, and (as of 2026-07-25) their clearances no longer converge
  into a single joint output either — each issues independently.
