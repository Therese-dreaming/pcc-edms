> **2026-07-02:** Reconciled against `reqs/DPO EFORM 1 SAMPLE.pdf` (the actual, EVP-approved
> intake form) and `0.4-dpo-ethics-integration.md`. **This is not a DPREQ-only form** — Form 1 is

## Purpose
Captures data needed for DPO (and, via the same submission, the Ethics/REC track) to review
research/data-collection activity **before** it begins. One submission serves both tracks — see
`0.4-dpo-ethics-integration.md`.

### 🟢 Section B — Study Information (source: Form 1, confirmed)

### 🟢 Review Checklist (source: Form 1, confirmed — Yes/No/Not Applicable per item)
1. Minors involved as participants? (below 18) — if Yes, requires parental consent sample letter
   upload + scanned reply slips upload
2. Approved letter signed by the head of target respondents on file? — if No, requires a formal
   request letter, later the approved/scanned version
3. Will the study involve voluntary participation of all respondents?
4. Will participants' identities and responses remain confidential?
5. Will participants be free to withdraw anytime without penalty?
6. Will the study avoid exposing participants to harm or risk?
7. Will collected data be used strictly for academic purposes?
8. Confirmation that required documents (§ below) are uploaded to the EDMS

## Output
On approval **by both tracks**, the system generates the **joint Data Privacy and Research
Ethics Clearance Certificate** (Form 3; PDF, auto-named per `4.2-file-management-naming.md`),
co-signed by the **DPO Officer** and the **Research Ethics Head**. Per
`0.4-dpo-ethics-integration.md`, the certificate is not released/downloadable until both
signatures are present. It carries a unique clearance number, applicant details, validity
period, both signatures, and a **QR Verification Code** linking to the public verification
portal (see `3.1-remis-application-form.md`).
