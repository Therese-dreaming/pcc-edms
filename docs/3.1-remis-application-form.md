## Purpose
Captures data for research ethics review by the REC — submission, review, approval,
monitoring, tracking, and archiving. Runs as the Ethics track of the single application
described in `0.4-dpo-ethics-integration.md`, in parallel with the DPO track (`1.x`).

## Output
On approval by the Ethics track, the Ethics Committee Chair (Form 3's "Research Ethics Head" —
see `0.4-dpo-ethics-integration.md` for the open question on whether these are the same role)
signs the **joint** Data Privacy and Research Ethics Clearance Certificate (Form 3) — the same
certificate the DPO track (`1.1`) produces its half of, not a separate REMIS-only certificate.
Per `0.4`, the certificate is withheld from the applicant until the DPO track has also signed.
It contains (FRS §XI, confirmed, plus the DPO co-signature per Form 3):
- Clearance Number (format `REC-YYYY-NNNN`, see `3.3`)
- Study Title
- Researcher Names
- Approval Date
- Validity Period
- Authorized Signatures — **both** DPO Officer and Research Ethics Head/Ethics Committee Chair
- **QR Verification Code**, linking to a public **Verification Portal** where the clearance's
  authenticity and validity can be checked by number or QR scan without logging in

The certificate PDF is stored in the repository and becomes downloadable by the applicant only
once both signatures are present; the verification portal check is read-only and exposes no
personal data beyond clearance validity/status.
